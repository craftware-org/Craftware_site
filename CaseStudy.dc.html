PROJECT PREVIEW
01 — CHALLENGE

Quba International had built a strong offline reputation for curated travel packages, but its digital presence did not reflect it. There was no proper website, enquiries came only through phone calls, and the brand had no consistent visual identity across platforms. They needed a modern digital presence capable of presenting travel packages professionally and turning interest into booked enquiries.

02 — STRATEGY

We built a digital roadmap centred on one metric: qualified package enquiries. That meant a fast, conversion-focused website, a coherent brand system, and a paid social engine to drive demand.

—Package-first site architecture with enquiry capture on every page.
—Brand system: logo refinement, colour, typography, photo treatment.
—Always-on Meta Ads with seasonal package creative and retargeting.
03 — EXECUTION

Design, development, branding and campaigns ran in parallel. The site shipped in six weeks; the ad engine went live alongside it with a library of package-level creative.
